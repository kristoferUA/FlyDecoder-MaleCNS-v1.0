FlyDecoder application author: [kristoferUA](https://github.com/kristoferUA).

This application downloads and uses the following files from the official MaleCNS v1.0 release:

- `body-annotations-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather`
- `body-neurotransmitters-male-cns-v1.0.feather`
- `connectome-weights-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather`

Source: [MaleCNS download page](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) and its [official Google Cloud Storage release](https://storage.googleapis.com/flyem-male-cns/v1.0/connectome-data/flat-connectome/).
